The Story

The Tanaka family has grown strawberries in Yame for three generations. What began as a single greenhouse in 1992 grew, season by season, into a farm devoted entirely to the pursuit of the perfect berry — patient, exacting, and quietly proud.

Today the current generation continues that tradition, pairing hard-won family knowledge with careful innovation in temperature and humidity control. Every box that leaves the farm carries that history — and the human care behind it.
