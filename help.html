Category 01

Ordering & Reservation

How do I reserve a box for the next arrival?
Pick a box, choose your delivery option, and pay to confirm your reservation before the deadline shown for that lot. Once the arrival lands and clears customs, your box ships in the delivery window. Related: How do payments work? →
When does the next reservation window open?
Each lot opens for pre-order as soon as the Japan arrival is scheduled — join the newsletter to be notified the moment it does.
Can I modify or cancel my reservation?
Yes, up to the reservation deadline for that lot. See our cancellation policy for details after the deadline.
What payment methods do you accept?
GCash, Maya, and major credit/debit cards (Visa / Mastercard).
Do you accept GCash or bank transfer?
GCash and Maya are both supported at checkout, alongside cards.
Is there a minimum order amount?
No minimum — reserve a single box or as many as the lot allows.
Category 02

Delivery

When will the strawberries be delivered?
Typically 1–2 days after the berries land and clear customs — the exact window is shown when you reserve.
Which areas do you deliver to?
Metro Manila same-day, nearby provinces next-day, and select other cities in 2–3 days. See the Delivery & Freshness section above for the full list.
Can I choose the delivery date?
You can choose a preferred window at checkout where available; delivery is tied to each arrival to guarantee freshness.
Can I track my shipment?
Yes — you'll receive a tracking link about 24 hours before delivery.
Category 03

Products & Quality

What happens if the strawberries arrive damaged?
Contact us within 24 hours with a photo — we'll arrange a replacement or full refund under our freshness guarantee.
How long do strawberries stay fresh?
Best enjoyed within 2–3 days of delivery; refrigerate promptly and don't wash until ready to eat.
What varieties do you carry?
Amaou, Tochiotome, and seasonal specials like Skyberry, depending on the arrival.
