The journey

From Chiba to your door

Our strawberries are grown on two family farms in Chiba, Japan — then kept cold every step of the way to reach you at their freshest. Here is the full journey, start to finish.

~36–48h
Chiba, JP
Unbroken
1–2 days

Timings are illustrative and depend on the arrival, customs, and your delivery area.

Step by step

Six steps, one unbroken cold chain

Every stage is planned around one goal: keep the berries cold and handled gently until they reach you.

1
Step 1

Harvest at dawn

Berries are picked ripe in the cool of early morning, when sugars are highest and the fruit is firm.

2
Step 2

Sorted & chilled

At the farm they are hand-sorted, gently packed, and brought down to a cold holding temperature straight away.

3
Step 3

Cold transport

Refrigerated transport carries the reserved lot from Chiba to the airport without breaking the chill.

4
Step 4

Flown to Manila

The berries are air-freighted to the Philippines so they spend hours, not weeks, in transit.

5
Step 5

Customs & freshness check

On arrival they clear customs and pass a freshness check by our local import partner before release.

6
Step 6

Delivered to you

Chilled last-mile delivery brings your reserved box to your door, typically 1–2 days after landing.

Picked at dawn → at your door in ~36–48 hours.|Cold-chain the whole way. No weeks in a warehouse.
Why it stays fresh

What keeps every box at its best

Unbroken cold chain

From the farm chiller to your doorstep, the berries are kept cold at every hand-off.

Reserved by the lot

We buy small, specific lots to order — so nothing sits waiting, and freshness is planned in.

Careful handling

Hand-sorting and gentle packing protect delicate fruit through every leg of the trip.

Freshness check

Our local partner inspects each arrival before it is released for delivery.

What this means for you

Berries at their sweetestPicked ripe and kept cold, not ripened in transit.
