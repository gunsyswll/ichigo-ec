Amaou あまおう

Deep-crimson, honeyed and generous — the berry people ask for by name.

AmaouTochiotomeSkyberry
Single-origin · Chiba, Japan

“Round, glossy and almost jammy-sweet — Amaou is indulgence in a single bite.”

Amaou is the berry most people picture when they imagine a luxury Japanese strawberry. The fruit is large and rounded, with a deep-crimson skin that carries a lush, honeyed sweetness balanced by only the gentlest acidity.

The flesh is soft and melting rather than firm — it rewards eating soon after arrival, chilled and stems-on. It is the lot we reserve most often, and the one we reach for when a box needs to feel like a treat.
