Frequently asked questions

When will the strawberries be delivered?
Delivery follows each Japan arrival. Once you reserve, you'll see the exact delivery window — typically 1–2 days after the berries land and clear customs.
What happens if the strawberries arrive damaged?
Our freshness guarantee covers you: contact us within 24 hours with a photo and we'll arrange a replacement or full refund.
How does the subscription (Club) work?
The 頒布会 Club is three monthly deliveries, each a different seasonal variety chosen by the farmers. Reserve once, enjoy three arrivals.
Can I choose the delivery date?
Delivery is tied to each Japan arrival to guarantee freshness, but you can select your preferred window at checkout where available.
Can I send this as a gift?
Yes — the Premium Gift Box is made for it, and you can ship to any address in our delivery area with a personal note.
