The Story

The Tanaka family has grown strawberries in Yame for three generations. What began as a single greenhouse in 1992 grew, season by season, into a farm devoted entirely to the pursuit of the perfect berry — patient, exacting, and quietly proud.

Today the current generation continues that tradition, pairing hard-won family knowledge with careful innovation in temperature and humidity control. Every box that leaves the farm carries that history — and the human care behind it.

Craft

Growing method & philosophy

Soil & Water

A carefully managed soil and water balance, unique to this farm, builds the berry's depth of flavour.

Temperature Control

Greenhouse temperature and humidity are tuned through the season for optimum sweetness.

Hand-Picked Selection

Each berry is hand-picked at peak ripeness and inspected before it's ever packed.

"We grow the strawberry we'd want to give our own family — nothing less leaves this farm."— Tanaka-san, Farm A

The Story

The Sato family has tended Sun Harvest since 1985, building a quiet reputation for berries as beautiful as they are sweet. Two generations have refined a single craft: coaxing colour, shape, and balance from each variety, row by row.

Their approach pairs advanced cultivation with old-fashioned patience — light, climate, and selection tuned so that every Tochiotome and Skyberry is chosen for shine and flavour before it is ever packed.

Craft

Growing method & philosophy

Variety Selection

Only the varieties that travel and taste best — Tochiotome for balance, Skyberry for size and shine.

Climate Tuning

Light and humidity are adjusted through the season for even ripening and deep, uniform colour.

Careful Selection

Every berry is graded by shape, shine, and sweetness before it earns a place in the box.

"A beautiful berry earns its place on the table before the first bite."— Sato-san, Farm B
