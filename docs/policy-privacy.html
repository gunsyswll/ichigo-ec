Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Order information — the products you buy, order value, order date, and any notes you add at checkout.
  • Contact information — name, email address, and phone number.
  • Delivery address — the Philippine address (and, where relevant, building/unit and delivery instructions) we ship your order to.
  • Account information — if you create a customer account, your login details and saved addresses.
  • Subscription information — if you join Ichigo Club, your delivery schedule and recurring order preferences.
  • Gift details — if you send a gift, the recipient's name, an optional personal message (up to 200 characters), your ribbon choice, and a requested delivery date. This is personal information about someone other than you — if you give it to us, please make sure they're comfortable with you sharing it so we can prepare and deliver their gift.
  • Support and claims correspondence — messages you send us, including any photos you submit when reporting an issue under our freshness guarantee.

Payment information

We do not see or store your full card number. Payment is handled by third-party payment processor(s) that we connect to this store's checkout. Those processors collect and process your payment details directly, under their own privacy policies. The payment methods available to you are whatever is shown on the checkout page and on our Help page at the time you order.

Information collected automatically

Like most online stores, this site uses cookies and similar technologies (see "Cookies" below) to keep your cart working, protect against fraud, and understand how the store is used. This can include your IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, and referring pages. This site also loads its fonts directly from Google Fonts on every page; that connection shares your IP address with Google as part of delivering the font files. Items you add to your wishlist are saved only in your own browser's local storage on your device — we do not receive or store that information ourselves.

Information from other sources

We receive information from Shopify (our e-commerce platform), our payment processor(s), and the courier that delivers your order, as needed to process and fulfil your purchase. If you sign up through the "Get notified" popup on this site, that signup is handled by Shopify's Forms app, installed in our store admin, which collects your email address for that purpose.

How we use your information

  • To take, process, and fulfil your order, including cross-border shipment from Japan to the Philippines.
  • To communicate with you about your order, delivery windows, and any freshness-guarantee claim.
  • To manage your Ichigo Club subscription, including recurring billing and scheduled deliveries.
  • To prevent fraud and keep checkout secure.
  • To comply with tax, customs, and other legal obligations in Japan and the Philippines.
  • To operate and improve this store, including basic analytics on how it's used.
  • To send you marketing communications where you've opted in, and to let you opt out at any time.

Legal bases for processing

We process your personal data on one or more of these grounds: performance of the contract when you place an order or subscribe to Ichigo Club; compliance with legal obligations (for example, tax and customs record-keeping); your consent (for example, marketing emails, which you can withdraw at any time); and our legitimate interests in running and securing this store, balanced against your rights. Where Japanese law requires a different basis for a particular use (for example, third-party data sharing), we rely on the applicable exception under the APPI, such as outsourcing to a processor acting under our instructions.

  • Payment processor(s) — receive your payment details directly to authorize and settle your transaction.
  • Couriers / logistics partners — receive the delivery recipient's name, address, and phone number to deliver your order (for gift orders, this is the gift recipient's details). Our carrier for this route is [COURIER NAME].
