Our Freshness Guarantee

Every Ichigo order is covered by our 100% Freshness Guarantee. If your strawberries arrive less than perfect, tell us within 24 hours of delivery (Philippine time) and we will make it right — a replacement box on the next available arrival, or a full refund. That is a promise, not a suggestion, and this policy exists to spell out exactly how it works.

Strawberries are a fresh, perishable product. Once delivered, we cannot resell or restock them, so we cannot accept them back as a physical return. That is normal for perishable food and does not reduce your guarantee in any way — it just means we settle a valid claim with photos rather than a mailed-back box. You do not need to ship anything to us, and you do not need to keep the berries once your claim is resolved.

How to Claim Under the Guarantee

  1. Act within 24 hours of delivery (Philippine time). This is the issue window advertised on our product pages and in our FAQ, and we hold ourselves to it.
  2. Contact us at [SUPPORT EMAIL] (or [SUPPORT PHONE], if you'd rather call) with your order number.
  3. Include photos of the strawberries and, where relevant, the box and packaging — this lets us confirm the issue and process your claim quickly without asking you to send anything back.
  4. We aim to respond within 24 hours, the same turnaround promised on our Help page, and to confirm a replacement or refund as soon as we've reviewed your photos.

What's Covered

  • Damaged or spoiled berries — crushed, moldy, leaking, or otherwise arrived in poor condition.
  • Quality below our standard — for example, visibly unripe or overripe fruit inconsistent with a fresh Japan arrival.
  • Wrong item — you received a different box, variety, or quantity than what you reserved.
  • Non-delivery — your order never arrived, or was lost or significantly delayed in transit through no fault of yours.

What's Not Covered

  • Change of mind. Because strawberries are perishable and shipped cold-chain specifically for your order, we can't accept a "no longer want it" return once a reservation ships.
  • Normal, minor cosmetic variation between berries — hand-picked fruit isn't uniform, and small differences in size or shape aren't a quality issue.

Replacement or Refund

For a valid claim, you can choose a replacement or a refund. If the same box or variety isn't available for the next arrival, we'll issue a full refund instead.

How and When Refunds Are Issued

Refunds are issued to the payment method you used at checkout. Your order is charged in Philippine pesos (PHP) — our store's base currency is Japanese yen, but checkout automatically converts and settles in PHP for every buyer — so refunds are issued in PHP, for the same amount you were charged, back to that original payment method. If we're ever unable to return funds to your original payment method, we'll contact you to arrange another way to refund you.

A full refund under the Freshness Guarantee covers the product price you paid for that order. Cancelling a Reservation Before the Lot Deadline

Each box is tied to a specific Japan arrival with its own reservation deadline (shown at checkout, and typically Monday 23:59 PHT for that week's lot). You can modify or cancel your reservation any time before that deadline: [CANCELLATION REFUND TERMS].

Once the deadline passes, your reservation is locked in — that's the point at which we commit the order into that lot's harvest, cold-chain, and delivery schedule, so we're not able to cancel or refund a reservation after its deadline except under the Freshness Guarantee once the box has arrived.

Ichigo Club Subscription

The Ichigo Club delivers once a month, each time a different seasonal variety. It is an ongoing plan: it bills and ships automatically every month and keeps going until you cancel — there is no fixed number of deliveries and no end date. You can pause, skip or cancel anytime from your account; cancel before a cycle's reservation deadline if you don't want that cycle to bill.

  • Cancel anytime from your account. Cancelling before a cycle's reservation deadline stops that cycle from being charged or shipped, and any amount already charged for that not-yet-shipped cycle is refunded in full.
  • Already-delivered cycles are treated like any other order — not refundable for change of mind, but fully covered by the Freshness Guarantee if a delivery arrives damaged, spoiled, or wrong, following the same 24-hour claim process above.
  • Undelivered future cycles are simply never charged once you cancel — there's nothing to refund because nothing was billed yet.

Non-Returnable, By Design

To be direct about it: because our product is fresh perishable food shipped cold-chain for a specific delivery, we do not accept returned strawberries, and we don't ask you to send anything back. The Freshness Guarantee is how we make that fair — a fast photo-based claim, and a replacement or full refund within the 24-hour window, rather than a return process that wouldn't make sense for a perishable box.
