Shipping Policy

Ichigo ships premium Japanese strawberries by air from Japan. We currently deliver to the Philippines only. You're welcome to order from anywhere in the world, but the delivery address on your order must be inside the Philippines — we cannot ship to any other country at this time.

Delivery areas

Coverage and delivery speed vary by area. The exact areas we can currently reach are:

Metro Manila

Makati, BGC, Ortigas, Quezon City, Pasig, Mandaluyong, Manila, San Juan, Parañaque, Las Piñas, Muntinlupa. Same-day delivery is available here, on the delivery day for the lot you reserved.

Nearby provinces

Cavite, Laguna, Rizal, Bulacan. Delivery here typically runs one day behind Metro Manila (next-day).

Other cities (limited)

Cebu, Davao, Iloilo, Baguio. These take longer — around 2–3 days — and a surcharge applies.

If your city isn't listed above, use the ZIP/city check on our Delivery & Freshness page, or contact us before ordering to confirm we can reach you. We'd rather tell you up front than have a box arrive somewhere we can't properly keep it cold.

How the reservation-to-delivery cycle works

Because the strawberries are picked in Japan to order, you're not buying from a box already sitting in a warehouse — you're reserving a place in the next incoming lot. Each lot has a reservation deadline; orders placed after a deadline are handled on the next lot — we'll confirm the details with you.

A typical cycle looks like this (the exact dates for your lot are shown when you reserve, and can shift — see "Delays outside our control" below):

  • Reservation deadline — the cutoff to join that lot, shown at checkout.
  • Harvest & quality check in Japan — berries are hand-picked and inspected shortly after the deadline closes.
  • Air freight, Japan → Philippines — the lot travels cold-chain, by air.
  • Arrival in the Philippines, customs clearance, and local quality check — the lot lands and is cleared before anything goes out for delivery.
  • Final-mile delivery, 1–2 days after the lot clears customs — Metro Manila first, then nearby provinces, then other covered cities.

If you're on an Ichigo Club subscription, each of your three monthly deliveries goes through this same cycle separately — a fresh reservation deadline, a fresh lot, and a fresh delivery window each month, rather than one large shipment.

Cold-chain handling

Strawberries are perishable, so we keep the cold chain intact the whole way from Japan to your door:

  • Berries are kept in the 2–8°C range from packing through delivery.
  • Boxes are insulated (foam with reflective lining) and packed with gel ice packs.
  • Berries sit in an individual cup tray, single layer, so they aren't stacked or bruised in transit.
  • You'll receive photo documentation of the quality checks done in Japan, on arrival, and before your box goes out for delivery.

Because of this, a box can't sit unattended for long after it arrives — see the next two sections.

Someone needs to be available to receive your delivery

Because this is a cold-chain, perishable delivery, please make sure someone is available at the delivery address during the window we give you (generally 9am–6pm in Metro Manila, 8am–6pm in the provinces, and [OTHER CITIES DELIVERY WINDOW] in Cebu, Davao, Iloilo, and Baguio, on the delivery day for your lot). Please double-check that your delivery address and phone number are correct and reachable — a courier who can't reach you or find the address can cost the shipment valuable cold-chain time.

Delays outside our control

Harvest, customs clearance, and air freight are all steps we don't fully control. Weather, transport delays and customs clearance can all push a lot's arrival and delivery back. When that happens, we'll update you as soon as we know, and your delivery window will move accordingly. Our Freshness 100% Guarantee still covers the condition of your strawberries when they arrive — contact us within 24 hours with a photo and we'll arrange a replacement or full refund. That guarantee is about the condition of the berries, not the length of a delay itself when the cause is outside our control.

Tracking your order

You'll receive a tracking link roughly 24 hours before your delivery window, once your lot has cleared customs and is on its way to you.

Customs and import duties

Your box travels from Japan and goes through import clearance in the Philippines before it's released for final-mile delivery — that's the "customs cleared" step in the cycle above.
