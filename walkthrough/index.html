Ichigo ／苺

Website Walkthrough

Premium Japanese strawberries — pre-order & 頒布会 subscription — delivered fresh to the Philippines. A page-by-page guide to the storefront and what every section does.

Design preview · for client review

What this is

A custom storefront in the brand's image-led, "ちょこっと贅沢 (a little luxury)" style — simple, professional and approachable. Palette: Ripe Red, warm off-white and greige; type: Libre Baskerville + Outfit. The site centres on lot-based pre-orders, the monthly 頒布会 club, and farmer storytelling with QR traceability.

Pages

  • Home — The storefront's front door — sets the premium-but-approachable tone and funnels visitors to reserve the next arrival.
  • Shop / Reserve — The catalogue — browse and reserve from the current and upcoming lots.
  • Farmers — Builds trust — the people and places behind every box (the 親近感 pillar).
  • Farm detail — A deep-dive on a single farm — story, method, and its boxes.
  • About — The company story and the import/logistics credibility.
  • FAQ — Self-service answers to reduce pre-purchase friction.
  • Delivery & Freshness — Sets delivery expectations and reassures on freshness — key for a pre-paid, perishable product.
  • Product detail — The conversion page — everything needed to reserve one box.
  • 404 (not found) — A friendly, on-brand dead-end that routes lost visitors back to shopping.

Note: all photography shown is temporary placeholder imagery — final shots and the real farm details (names, varieties, Chiba farms) will replace them.
